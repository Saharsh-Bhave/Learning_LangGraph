Good:
I’ve tried three different brands this year, and this is the first one that doesn’t lose its mind when the Wi-Fi hiccups. The 4K resolution is crisp enough to see the neighborhood cat’s whiskers, and the app interface is actually intuitive. I was worried the setup would be a nightmare, but I had it mounted and synced in under 10 minutes. It’s rare to find hardware that feels this premium without a massive monthly subscription fee. Highly recommend if you’re tired of "smart" tech that makes you feel dumb.

Bad:

The build quality of the camera is top-tier, and the night vision is honestly the best I’ve seen. However, I’ve noticed a consistent "communication gap" between the unit and the cloud. I tested it by walking onto my porch, and the alert didn't hit my phone until I was already back inside and halfway through making a sandwich.
It’s a bit frustrating because you want to be able to react to things as they happen, right? If someone is at the door, knowing about it four minutes later doesn't really help the situation. I’m hoping a firmware update fixes this soon, because right now, it feels like I’m watching a delayed broadcast of my own life.

I really liked the design of this system, but I’ve hit a wall. Every time the temperature drops below freezing, the live feed starts stuttering and eventually just cuts out entirely. I live in a climate where it’s cold six months of the year, so having a "security" system that takes a nap when the weather gets tough is a major problem for me.
The tone of the support team was polite, but they didn't have a real fix other than "restarting the hub," which only works until the next frost. I need something reliable 24/7, not just when the sun is out. I’ll be returning this and looking for a more rugged alternative.
